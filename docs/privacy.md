counts no visits and shows no ads.

## Fonts and images

Fonts and badges are downloaded when the site is built and served from here.
Your browser does not connect to Google or any other provider for them.

## Storage in your browser

The site remembers a few settings in your browser's local storage: the colour
scheme, the tab you picked (such as Spoolman or FilaMan) and, on the
[Recommended printers](use/printers.md) page, the Amazon shop you picked. These
values never leave your browser, and you can delete them at any time in your
browser's settings. They only serve the function you chose yourself (Section
25(2) No. 2 TDDDG).

To suggest a shop, the site also reads your browser's time zone and language.
This, too, happens only in your browser; nothing is sent.

## Embedded content

The [Wiring](build/wiring.md) page embeds an interactive diagram from Cirkit
Designer. When you open that page, your browser loads it directly from
app.cirkitdesigner.com, which tells the provider your IP address. The
[Cirkit Designer privacy policy](https://www.cirkitdesigner.com/privacy-policy.html)
applies.

## Links to other sites

Links lead to GitHub, Discord, Ko-fi, MakerWorld, AliExpress and Amazon, among
others. Only when you click one do you leave this site, and there the privacy
policy of that provider applies.
